# `Translate`-extension obstructions for $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{equi}}$ and $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{rel}}$

**Issue**: ISSUE-011 (Step 4)

**Goal**: per algebra, identify which `Translate`-signature extensions would
absorb the counterexample candidates of §3 in

## 1. Recap: the five $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{PWR}}$ obstructions

From the paper's Table~\ref{tab:five-obstructions} (§subsec:negative-pwr):

1. Operator-spectrum output: $k_{\mathrm{eff}}$ is an *eigenvalue*, not in $\mathcal{Y}$.
2. Homomorphism-failure $\pi$-template: $d\rho$ is not a semigroup
   homomorphism on $\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{rod}}$.
3. Configuration-indexed adjoint: $\phi^{\dagger}_X$ varies with $X$.
4. Higher-order mixed-difference $\pi$: $\partial^{2}\rho/\partial T_{\mathrm{mod}}\partial C_{B}$.
5. Two-direction joint parametric dependence: $(T_{\mathrm{mod}}, C_{B})$ jointly.

Each row is an independent extension dimension. Below we identify the
analogous obstructions on the other two algebras.

## 2. $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{equi}}$ obstructions

| Counterexample | Failure mode | Required `Translate`-extension | Closure / decidability compatibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\rho_{\mathrm{compose}}$ (§3.1 equi) | $\pi$-template enumerates a *single group's* orbit; the compositional MR requires the *direct-product orbit* $G_1 \times G_2$ acting on $\mathcal{X}$ | **(E1) Product-group $\pi$-template**: $\pi \subseteq (\mathcal{X}\times\mathcal{Y})^{|G_1| \times |G_2|}$ with tuple generated by $(g_1, g_2) \mapsto g_1 g_2 \cdot x_0$ | Theorem~\ref{thm:closure} preserved: the product group is a single group, and the new MR is still single-block-derivable from $G$ once the block contains $G_1 \times G_2$. Theorem~\ref{thm:decidable} preserved: cardinality $|G_1|\cdot|G_2|$ is polynomial in input size when both groups are finite or finite-dimensional |
| $\rho_{\mathrm{gauge}}$ (§3.2 equi) | $\pi$-template is parametrised by *group elements*; gauge equivariance requires parametrisation by *sections of a principal $H$-bundle*, i.e.\ by a function on the input domain | **(E2) Bundle-section $\pi$-template**: $\pi$ takes a *gauge field* $\mathbf{g} \in \mathcal{C}(M,\,H)$ as parameter, with consistency required across chart transition functions $g_{p \to q}$ | Theorem~\ref{thm:closure} preserved provided the bundle is *trivialisable* or sectional support is enumerated by a finite atlas (e.g.\ the icosahedral atlas with 12 charts $\times$ 60-element symmetry). Theorem~\ref{thm:decidable}: polynomial when atlas size and gauge enumeration are bounded; non-trivial when $M$ has unbounded topology |

The two extensions are **independent**:

- E1 (product-group) does not absorb E2 (gauge-bundle): a gauge field
  $\mathbf{g}$ is a *section*, not a tuple of group elements; a product
  group is a single group, with a single group action on the input.
- E2 (gauge-bundle) does not absorb E1 (product-group): a gauge field
  on a product space is still a single section, but it does not encode
  the joint constraint $f(R\,P\,x) = \rho_{\mathrm{out}}(R)\,f(x)$
  on the *commutator* of two group actions.

The independence claim mirrors the five-row independence of
Table~\ref{tab:five-obstructions} ($\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{PWR}}$).

## 3. $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{rel}}$ obstructions

| Counterexample | Failure mode | Required `Translate`-extension | Closure / decidability compatibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\rho_{\mathrm{agg\text{-}proj}}$ (§5 rel; primary candidate) | $\mathcal{B}^{*}_{\mathrm{rel}}$'s `Translate` produces single-rewrite-step identities. The MR requires a *lift* of an aggregate $\mathrm{Agg}_f$ through projection $\pi$, which is a *two-block composition* of $\mathcal{B}^{*}_{\mathrm{rel}}$ (rewrite identity) and an aggregate-algebra step | **(E3) Aggregate-as-algebra $\pi$-template**: $\pi$ admits aggregates modelled as semilattice / monoid homomorphisms on the projection lattice, not as uninterpreted functions | Theorem~\ref{thm:closure} preserved provided the aggregate algebra is a finite set of homomorphism templates (sum, count, min, max, bit-AND/OR; *not* arbitrary UDFs). Theorem~\ref{thm:decidable} preserved at the same complexity since each homomorphism check is constant-time once the aggregate's algebra type is known |
| testAggregateConstantKeyRule / testDecorrelateAggWithConstantGroupKey | Joint use of $\mathcal{B}^{*}_{\mathrm{rel}}$ (constant-key rewrite) and $O_{\le}$ (integrity-constraint monotonicity) | **(E4) Constraint-aware $\pi$-template**: $\pi$ admits integrity constraints (uniqueness, foreign-key, CHECK) as $O_{\le}$-block partial-order generators, with the rewrite identity then propagating through them | Theorem~\ref{thm:closure} preserved with caveat: the constraint set must be finite (TPC-H-class normal-form constraints). Theorem~\ref{thm:decidable}: each constraint check is polynomial; no fundamental blow-up |
| testCustomColumnResolvingInCorrelatedSubQuery | Three-valued logic over NULLs is not in $\mathcal{B}^{*}_{\mathrm{rel}}$'s idempotent semiring | **(E5) Three-valued-logic $\pi$-template**: $\pi$ admits values in $\{\bot,\,\top,\,\mathrm{null}\}$ with explicit null-propagation rules | Theorem~\ref{thm:closure} preserved: the three-valued semiring is *still* idempotent (extends the two-valued one); $\mathcal{B}^{*}_{\mathrm{rel}}$'s closure proof transfers. Theorem~\ref{thm:decidable}: constant-time check per cell |

The three extensions are pairwise independent:

- E3 (aggregate-as-algebra) does not absorb E4 (constraint-aware): a
  constant-key removal is *not* an aggregate; it is an integrity-constraint
  pruning.
- E4 does not absorb E3: aggregating sums is not a constraint propagation.
- E5 (three-valued) is orthogonal to both E3 and E4: a UNION-ALL with NULL
  semantics requires three-valued logic but not aggregation, and an
  aggregate over a constant-key does not require NULL three-valued logic
  in standard SQL semantics.

## 4. Cross-algebra summary

| Algebra | Extensions identified | Independent from PWR extensions? |
|---|---|---|
| $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{PWR}}$ (paper §subsec:negative-pwr) | 5 (operator-spectrum output, homomorphism failure, configuration-indexed adjoint, mixed-difference, joint parametric) | --- |
| $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{equi}}$ (this issue) | 2 (E1 product-group $\pi$; E2 bundle-section $\pi$) | E1 specialises PWR-4 (mixed-difference $\to$ product-group); E2 specialises PWR-3 (configuration-indexed $\to$ section-indexed). Both are properly stronger than PWR-3/4 in the sense that the algebraic primitive (group orbit vs.\ bundle section) is type-distinct from $\phi^{\dagger}$'s history-indexing |
| $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{rel}}$ (this issue) | 3 (E3 aggregate-algebra; E4 constraint-aware; E5 three-valued logic) | All three are independent from PWR-1..5: relational algebra has no operator-spectrum (PWR-1), no homomorphism on a Lie semigroup (PWR-2), no $\phi^{\dagger}$ adjoint structure (PWR-3), no continuous-parameter mixed derivative (PWR-4), no continuous parametric directions (PWR-5) |

**Independence across algebras**: the seven new extensions
(E1, E2, E3, E4, E5 + two by-transfer) are pairwise independent.
The total `Translate`-extension dimensionality identified by the three
algebra surveys is therefore $5 + 2 + 3 = 10$ (over PWR + equi + rel,
modulo the two E1/E2-to-PWR-3/4 specialisations, which still merit
separate template entries because the algebraic primitive differs).

## 5. Implications for §subsec:third-domain's open-question paragraph

- $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{equi}}$: not closed under Theorem 1'. Two
  pairwise-independent counterexample candidates were identified
  ($\rho_{\mathrm{compose}}$ and $\rho_{\mathrm{gauge}}$), drawn from
  published equivariant-ML literature (EGNN \cite{Satorras2021EGNN};
  gauge-equivariant CNNs \cite{Cohen2019Gauge, Haan2020GaugeMesh}).
  Conjecture "the SO(3) Lie-algebra structure forces closure" is **not
  supported** by the survey; the counterexamples come from the
  *product-group* and *bundle-section* structure, which the
  Lie-algebra alone does not absorb.
- $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{rel}}$: not closed under Theorem 1'. Five
  counterexample candidates identified from the QED test suite, with
  $\rho_{\mathrm{agg\text{-}proj}}$ (Aggregate--Project transposition)
  selected as the primary witness. Conjecture "the idempotent-semiring
  structure admits at least one rewrite-template counterexample" is
  **supported and strengthened**: the counterexamples are not in the
  semiring's rewrite templates alone, they are in the *composition*
  of $\mathcal{B}^{*}_{\mathrm{rel}}$ with $O_{\le}$ (constraint-aware)
  or with an aggregate-algebra ninth block.

The paper-side §subsec:third-domain open-question paragraph should be
updated with this verdict; see step 5 of ISSUE-011 for the paragraph text.

## 6. Open items

- **Formal proofs** of the two-block (or block + extension) non-derivability
  for each candidate. The exploratory sketches above identify which
  block templates fail; a formal proof would exhaust the
  per-block-and-extension `Translate` templates analogously to
  Appendix~C.6 of the paper.
- **Closure preservation proofs** for each `Translate`-extension. The
  table above asserts compatibility on intuitive grounds; a
  Theorem~\ref{thm:closure}-style argument that each extension
  preserves single-block uniqueness under the canonical-block ordering
  is committed as follow-up.
- **Decidability complexity bounds** for each extension. The current
  Theorem~\ref{thm:decidable}'s $O(n\cdot t_{\max} \cdot \log n)$
  bound depends on the generator set being finite. Some extensions
  (E2 bundle-section, E5 three-valued logic on unbounded NULL count)
  may require an explicit bound argument.

These are the principal open theoretical questions a Composite-`Translate`
extension paper would need to answer; they are tracked as follow-up
beyond ISSUE-011's exploratory scope.
