\paragraph{Two-layer model (L1 MetaPatterns, L2 MR families).}
Let $\AP=(\mathcal{O},\circ,\sim_{\mathcal{F}})$ be the program-induced operator
algebra and $\Dec$ its structural decomposition.
\emph{Layer~1 (MetaPattern).} A \emph{MetaPattern} is the equivalence class of
algebra-induced metamorphic relations that \textsc{Construct-MP} derives, through
\Translate, from one minimal algebraic structure of $\AP$ (its generating basis).
The five MetaPatterns $\minv,\mmono,\madj,\mrev,\mconv$ are generated respectively
by a group action ($\Grp$), a partial order ($\Ord$), a self-adjoint operator
($\Sadj$), a time-reversal involution ($\Trev$), and a parametrised limit
($\Lim$); their set is $\MM$.
\emph{Layer~2 (MR family).} An \emph{MR family} is the set of executable
metamorphic relations obtained by instantiating one MetaPattern $m\in\MM$ under a
fixed $\pi$-template and a chosen MR mode (Mode-I input-orbit / Mode-M
implementation-orbit) through \Translate, i.e.\ $\Translate(\iota,s)$ for a
component invariant $\iota=(\Phi,\pi)$. The map from $\MM$ to families is
one-to-many: $\Grp\to\{a,b\}$ (equivariance, conservation),
$\Sadj\to\{c,d\}$ (self-adjoint, adjoint-duality), $\Trev\to\{e\}$ (time-reversal),
$\Ord\to\{f,g\}$ (static-order, dynamic-shape), and
$\Lim\to\{h,i,j\}$ (convergence, accuracy-order, representation-invariance).%
}
