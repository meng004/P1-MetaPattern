\providecommand{\TwoLayerModel}{%
\paragraph{Two-layer model (L1 MetaPatterns, L2 MR families).}
Let $\AP=(\mathcal{O},\circ,\sim_{\mathcal{F}})$ be the program-induced operator
algebra and $\Dec$ its structural decomposition.
\emph{Layer~1 (MetaPattern).} A \emph{MetaPattern} is the equivalence class of
algebra-induced metamorphic relations that \textsc{Construct-MP} derives, through
\Translate, from one minimal algebraic structure of $\AP$ (its generating basis).
The five MetaPatterns---$\minv$ (Invariance), $\mmono$ (Monotonicity),
$\madj$ (Adjoint), $\mrev$ (Reversal), $\mconv$ (Convergence)---are generated
respectively by a group action ($\Grp$), a partial order ($\Ord$), a self-adjoint
operator ($\Sadj$), a time-reversal involution ($\Trev$), and a parametrised limit
($\Lim$); their set is $\MM$.
\emph{Layer~2 (MR family).} An \emph{MR family} is the set of executable
metamorphic relations obtained by instantiating one MetaPattern $m\in\MM$ under a
fixed $\pi$-template and a chosen MR mode (Mode-I input-orbit / Mode-M
implementation-orbit) through \Translate, i.e.\ $\Translate(\iota,s)$ for a
component invariant $\iota=(\Phi,\pi)$. Each family is written
$\mathsf{f}_{\mathrm{parent}.\mathrm{child}}$ (sans-serif family glyph; the L1
parent stem precedes the dot). The map from $\MM$ to families is one-to-many:
$\Grp\to\{\finveqv\ (a,\text{equivariance}),\ \finvcon\ (b,\text{conservation})\}$,
$\Sadj\to\{\fadjself\ (c,\text{self-adjoint}),\ \fadjdual\ (d,\text{adjoint-duality})\}$,
$\Trev\to\{\frevtraj\ (e,\text{trajectory-reversal})\}$,
$\Ord\to\{\fmonostat\ (f,\text{static-order}),\ \fmonoshape\ (g,\text{dynamic-shape})\}$, and
$\Lim\to\{\fconvlim\ (h,\text{convergence}),\ \fconvrate\ (i,\text{accuracy-order}),\ \fconvrepr\ (j,\text{representation-invariance})\}$.%
}
