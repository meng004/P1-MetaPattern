**Detailed free-form panel report**

**EIC lens (scope, originality, significance, structure).**  
The paper sits at the intersection of metamorphic testing, formal methods, and empirical SE— squarely inside TOSEM's remit. The methodological move (lifting induction from per-MR enumeration to per-domain algebra identification) is clever and the NOETHER architecture diagram (Figure 1) is clear. However, the net contribution over existing meta-pattern catalogues (Ying et al. 2025 family-tree, METRIC+/METRIC, Segura's relational patterns, Zhou's symmetry MRP, MemoRIA, GenMorph, LiTOSEM2025 survey) is narrower than claimed: it supplies a constructive operator-block layer plus closure/complexity results within a deliberately circumscribed Translate image. The empirical legs are pilot-scale (one EGNN model, 20 hand-constructed mutations explicitly matched to blocks; DeepCrime pilot n=5; METRIC+ mapping on author-reimplemented Sun 2021 subjects). The reactor-physics validation re-uses the authors' own prior inductive catalogue, creating a circularity that the self-disclosed "prediction caveat" (Section 3.5) does not dispel. At ~18k words (even after appendix migration) the manuscript exceeds TOSEM's soft limit; several boundary-of-contribution tcolorboxes and repeated scope disclaimers add redundancy. Desk-reject risk on length and scope-overclaim is real. Recommendation: Major Revision with clear instructions to narrow claims, deliver independent external validation, and shorten.

**R1 lens (theory + statistics).**  
Theorem 1 is a by-construction well-formedness lemma for the downstream construction once the upstream block decomposition is given; marketing it as headline "no-drop closure invariant" inflates its status. Theorem 2's polynomial-time claim holds only under finite generating sets and output-polynomial enumeration when |G| is exponential (acknowledged in the complexity prose and Table 1 but not foregrounded in the abstract or contributions). The negative PWR result (Section 3.8, Appendix C.6) that falsifies the stronger absolute-completeness conjecture is the most substantive theoretical advance yet is presented as a secondary "negative instantiation." Statistical material in the case study and head-to-head is present (McNemar exact, Wilson CIs, Fleiss' κ on LLM audit) but omits multiplicity correction across the many pairwise tests, provides no power analysis for the n=20 / n=57 / n=120 experiments, and uses a mutation set explicitly constructed to cover one defect category per non-empty block—explicitly acknowledged as "construct-validity-controlled" yet still used to support H2. The gap between the elegant theory and the pilot-scale, partially self-referential evidence is the central methodological weakness. The D1/D2 stratification and L*-blindness prediction are clever but cannot rescue the design.

**R2 lens (MT/MR domain expert).**  
The paper correctly identifies the long-standing identification bottleneck and positions NOETHER as a structural alternative to inductive catalogues and search-based generators. However, engagement with the immediate prior art is insufficiently sharp: Gotlieb's symmetric testing (ISSRE 2003/2006), Segura's query-based and survey work, Zhou's symmetry MRP, Khritankov-Iakusheva's systematic grouping of input transformations, Patel-Hierons oracle strategies, Saha-Kanewala supervised MR, MemoRIA, Ying et al.'s 2025 family-tree formalism, and the recent LiTOSEM2025 survey are cited but the contrast paragraphs treat them primarily as inductive baselines rather than engaging their category-enumeration or mining machinery head-to-head. The METRIC+/METRIC mapping (Section 4.5) and the supplementary Path-A replication are valuable but still rely on author-reimplemented subjects. The claim that NOETHER "derives rather than repackages" symmetry intuitions is not yet substantiated beyond the internal PWR re-classification; the external industrial audit (S11) and cross-domain library instantiations (S12) are promising but remain secondary. Self-evaluation (EQ1/EQ3) is largely author-vs-author or LLM-panel-audited. The novelty delta must be tightened to "construction + proof over the operator-block layer" rather than a new foundational theory. The deflationary direction on PMCM (Section 4.7) is the most convincing practical contribution.

**R3 lens (equivariant ML + safety-critical V&V).**  
The Invariance-Blindness Theorem (Section 3.4) is the strongest cross-disciplinary result: it converts the by-construction closure into a falsifiable, non-tautological characterization of blind spots for linear operator faults. Its corollaries on single-block incompleteness and the complementarity of differential oracles are directly relevant to nuclear I&C and autonomous-system V&V. However, the theorem is proved only for exact-arithmetic linear faults; the floating-point regime (tolerance τ) and extension beyond {G,T*} are acknowledged as limitations but not quantified. The equivariant-ML instantiation (Section 3.6) and the reactor witness (LOCUST MTC-bor relation) are still largely self-derived; the industrial witness is valuable but does not constitute independent third-party V&V. GenMorph head-to-head loss on raw detection rate is candidly reported, yet claims of superior auditability/maintainability/reuse remain qualitative. Generalization from the three algebras to the claimed safety-critical target domains is not yet demonstrated at the scale or independence required for TOSEM. The five Translate obstructions identified in the negative PWR result are important for safety-critical completeness but left as open problems.
